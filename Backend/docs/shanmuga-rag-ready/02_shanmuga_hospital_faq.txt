QUESTION: Which packages are included under Master Health Check-Up Packages?
ANSWER: The Master Health Check-Up Packages category contains Silver Package at INR 1,650, Gold Package at INR 4,950, and Platinum Package at INR 7,980. Use the official English category and package names even in a Tamil response, and ask which package the caller wants explained.

QUESTION: What is the difference between the Silver Package and Gold Package?
ANSWER: Silver costs INR 1,650 and Gold costs INR 4,950. Both include CBC, Blood Grouping and RH Type, fasting and post-prandial sugar, HBA1C, Urea, Creatinine, Uric Acid, Lipid Profile, Liver Function Test, Urine Routine, ECG, Chest X-Ray, and one Doctor Consultation. Gold additionally includes Calcium, Iron and TIBC, Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, Homocysteine, TSH, T3, T4, Electrolytes, HSCRP, ECHO, USG Abdomen, and Dental Check-up.

QUESTION: What is the difference between the Gold Package and Platinum Package?
ANSWER: Gold costs INR 4,950 and Platinum costs INR 7,980. Platinum includes the main Gold tests and additionally includes ESR, HIV, HBSAG, HCV, PFT, Apolipoprotein A1 and B, Magnesium, Phosphorus, Carotid Doppler, Ankle Brachial Index, and Diabetic Foot Assessment. Gold includes one Doctor Consultation, while Platinum includes any four approved specialist consultations.
